3D ship flight controls

W / Space = forward thrust; S = reverse/brake. AD or ←→ = yaw. ↑↓ = pitch (climb/dive). QE = roll. Shift = boost (three available). P = pause; R = restart. The camera follows your ship as the world moves through simulation time.

What the fourth coordinate means

State = (x,y,z,t,velocity,orientation). Gravity from the orbiting Moon and asteroid positions depend explicitly on t, including while you maneuver in 3D. Pausing freezes physical time. The spacecraft has independently controlled yaw, pitch and roll. No extra spatial axis, relativistic time dilation, real solar-system prediction, stereoscopic VR, or multiplayer is claimed.
