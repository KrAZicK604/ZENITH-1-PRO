Dynamics and integrity

The spacecraft is now a genuine locally built 3D hull: layered metal fuselage, sweep wings, fins, raised canopy and three volumetric nozzles, shaded by WebGL2. Chase, orbit, top, side and front cameras plus wireframe and previous-time state ghosts are optional. The original 2D illustration remains a fallback on unsupported devices. The separate JPL planetarium uses real dated planetary positions but does not alter arcade physics. The fourth coordinate is elapsed simulation time, not a fourth spatial dimension. Fixed 60 Hz simulation, gravity in x/y/z, 3D asteroid fields, swept collision checks, bounded velocity and terminal crash/win states. All displayed coordinates and speed are game units, not kilometers or light speed. Local practice score only; no multiplayer, purchases or verified prizes.
