Scale and scientific integrity: This is a freely explorable, fictional 3D sandbox measured in game units (gu), not astronomical kilometers. The planetary markers are deliberately compressed to make exploration playable; they are not JPL-certified positions or physically realistic trajectories. A real Horizons/SPICE Mars-center numerical cross-check has passed at four discrete epochs, but that does not validate continuous orbital propagation, spacecraft transfer paths, fuel needs or independent physical measurements. The static reference values above never control this arcade flight. No payments, multiplayer, weapon systems, prizes, autonomous physical controls or production authority.
